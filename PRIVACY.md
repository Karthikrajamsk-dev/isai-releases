# Isai privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026 · applies to Isai 1.1.0 and later_

Isai is a personal project. It has **no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own.**

## What stays on your phone
- Your listening history (the *Your week* stats).
- Your settings, saved (offline) lyrics and lyric timing adjustments.
- The troubleshooting log.

Android's backup may copy your **settings and saved lyrics** to your Google account or a new phone. Listening history and the log are excluded.

## What goes online, and why
| Data | Sent to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Song title, artist, album, length | [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) | To find lyrics for the song playing |
| Lyric lines | Google Translate | Only if *English translation* is on |
| Nothing personal – a download request | GitHub | When you check for or download an update |

These services receive your IP address as part of any internet request. Isai sends no identifiers of its own.

## Permissions
| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Notification access | Read what your music app is playing (song, cover, controls). Only media sessions are used. |
| Notifications, full-screen notifications | Open the player on the lock screen. Silent. |
| Display over other apps | A backup way to open the player on some phones. |
| Battery optimisation exception | Keep Isai from being closed in the background. |
| Microphone (optional) | *Beat pulse* only: reads the music's output level. Nothing is recorded or saved. |
| Install apps | Install Isai updates you choose. |
| Internet | Lyrics, translation and update checks above. |

## Your choices
Translation and Beat pulse are off unless you turn them on. Isai checks for updates when you open its settings. Uninstalling Isai deletes all its data from your phone. *Settings → Backup* lets you export your settings and lyrics yourself.
